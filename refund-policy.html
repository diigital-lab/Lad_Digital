At LAD Digital, we take pride in the quality of our digital products. Please read this policy carefully before making a purchase, as it outlines the terms under which refunds may or may not be issued.

1. Nature of Digital Products

All products sold by LAD Digital are digital in nature — they are delivered electronically and made available for immediate download upon successful payment. Due to this, we are unable to accept returns or process refunds once the product has been delivered, as the product cannot be "returned" in the traditional sense.

All sales are considered final upon delivery of the digital product.

2. No Refund Policy

We do not offer refunds, cancellations, or exchanges in the following situations:

  • You changed your mind after purchase
  • You purchased the product by mistake
  • You found a similar product elsewhere at a lower price
  • You do not have the technical knowledge to use the product
  • You did not read the product description before purchasing

3. Eligible Exceptions

Refunds or replacements may only be considered in the following situations:

  • You did not receive the product due to a verified technical error on our end

If either of these applies to you, please contact us within 24–48 hours of your purchase with proof of the issue, and we will investigate promptly.
