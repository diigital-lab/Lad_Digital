Frequently Asked Questions

I didn't receive my download link — what do I do?
▾
First, check your spam or junk folder. If it's not there, email us at laddigital@zohomail.in with your payment reference and we'll resend it manually within hours.
How do I install a Shopify theme from the bundle?
▾
Go to your Shopify Admin → Online Store → Themes → Add theme → Upload ZIP file. Select your theme file and click Upload. No developer needed — it's drag-and-drop ready.
Can I use these themes on multiple stores?
▾
Yes. Your ₹149 purchase gives you a lifetime, unlimited-store licence. Use the themes on as many stores as you like — your own or your clients'.
Is there any physical delivery or shipping?
