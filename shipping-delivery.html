Instant Digital Delivery

⚡ LAD Digital sells exclusively digital products. There is no physical shipping involved. All products are delivered electronically — instantly, directly to you, upon successful payment confirmation.

1. Nature of Our Products

LAD Digital is a 100% digital store. We sell Shopify themes and digital resources that are delivered electronically. No physical items are manufactured, packaged, or shipped at any point in time.

This means:

  • There are no shipping charges — ever
  • There are no delivery timelines dependent on location or courier services
  • Your product is available to download immediately after payment
  • Delivery works the same whether you are in India or anywhere else in the world

2. How Digital Delivery Works

Once your payment is confirmed, here is exactly what happens:

1
Payment Confirmed

Your payment is processed securely through our trusted payment partner. You receive an on-screen confirmation immediately.

2
Download Link Generated

A secure, unique download link is automatically generated and sent to the email address you provided at checkout — typically within 1–2 minutes of payment.

3
Access Your Product

Click the download link in your email to access all 130+ premium Shopify themes. The download works on any device — phone, tablet, or laptop.

✓
Lifetime Access Secured

Your purchase grants you permanent, lifetime access. Save the files securely — there are no expiry dates and no re-download limits enforced arbitrarily.

3. Delivery Timeframe

Digital delivery is instant to near-instant in all cases. The expected timeframe after confirmed payment is:

  • On-screen access: Immediately after payment confirmation
  • Email with download link: Within 1–5 minutes
  • Maximum wait in exceptional cases: Up to 30 minutes

If you do not receive your download link within 30 minutes of payment, please check your spam or junk folder first, then contact us at laddigital@zohomail.in.

4. No Physical Shipping

LAD Digital does not ship any physical goods under any circumstances. If you are looking for a physical product delivery, please note that our store does not offer one. All purchases are for digital downloads only.

Accordingly:

  • No postal address is required from you
  • No tracking number or courier service is involved
  • No import duties, customs fees, or regional delivery charges apply
  • Delivery is identical for customers across India and internationally

5. Incorrect Email Address

Your download link is sent to the email address provided at checkout. Please ensure you enter a correct, active email address when completing your purchase.

LAD Digital is not responsible for non-delivery of the download link caused by an incorrect email address entered by the customer. If this happens, contact us promptly with your payment reference and we will resolve it manually.

6. Access Issues After Delivery

If you experience any difficulties downloading or accessing your product after receiving the link:

  • Try opening the link in a different browser
  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection
  • Check if the link has expired and contact us for a reissue

✅ We're here to help. If you face any issue with accessing your purchased product, reach out to us at laddigital@zohomail.in and our team will assist you promptly — usually within a few hours.

7. International Customers

Our digital products are available to customers worldwide. Since delivery is entirely digital, there are no geographic restrictions, no customs delays, and no additional charges for international customers. You receive the same instant access regardless of your location.
